The Shop

A neighborhood barbershop

South Side Barbershop is a local shop at 1509 East Carson Street, specializing in precision haircuts, beard trims and family-friendly service. Stop in for a clean fade, a scissor cut, a shape-up between visits, or a hot towel straight razor shave.
